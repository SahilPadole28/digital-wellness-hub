## 1. Overview & Testing Methodology

To satisfy mandatory requirement **R8 ("Every link tested; a link test record kept")**, all hyperlinks embedded across the Digital Wellness Resource Hub were systematically audited.

Links were tested via two parallel procedures:
1. **Automated Link Sweeping:** Script verification mapping internal routes and public endpoint availability.
2. **Manual Destination Verification:** Every vendor website, official documentation guide, privacy policy, and academic DOI link was manually opened in Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox to confirm the target page is live, serves valid content, and does not land on 404/broken errors or misleading affiliate redirects.
