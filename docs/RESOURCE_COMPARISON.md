## 1. Overview & Evaluation Framework

To fulfill **R2 ("8–12 useful resources in clear categories")** and **R3 ("Consistent comparison criteria for every resource")**, all 12 tools in the hub are systematically evaluated against standardized criteria:
1. **Purpose & Problem Solved**
2. **Supported Platforms & Device Compatibility**
3. **Cost Model & Paywalled Features (R4 Honesty Rule)**
4. **Friction Level & Psychological Intervention**
5. **Setup Duration**
6. **Privacy Requirements & Data Use**
7. **Accessibility Notes**
8. **Known Limitations & Honest Trade-offs**
9. **Relevant Academic Course Concept**
10. **Official Sources & Date Checked**

## 3. Quick Comparison Evaluation Lenses

For student convenience, the live website offers 4 curated lens presets:
1. **Built-in OS Controls (iOS Screen Time vs Android Digital Wellbeing):** Side-by-side comparison of factory-installed smartphone management tools.
2. **Study & Focus Blockers (Forest vs LeechBlock NG vs News Feed Eradicator):** Evaluates gamified loss aversion vs strict open-source domain lockouts vs social feed eradication.
3. **Bedtime & Sleep Routines (Sleep Focus vs Bedtime Mode vs f.lux):** Evaluates evening blue-light attenuation and automated Do Not Disturb routines across mobile and desktop.
4. **Online Privacy Trio (uBlock Origin vs DuckDuckGo vs Signal):** Comprehensive surveillance defense covering network tracker stripping, query anonymization, and end-to-end encrypted communication.
