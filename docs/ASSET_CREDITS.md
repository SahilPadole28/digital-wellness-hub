## 1. Asset Policy & Honesty Standards

In accordance with Section 2 (*What You Must Not Fabricate*) and R5/R10/R11 of the project specification:
1. **No Fake UIs:** No mockup or fake UI screenshots have been drawn.
2. **Official Extensions & Store Captures:** Extension store pages (Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons) and official websites (justgetflux.com) are captured directly via headless browser or sourced directly from verified developer listings (Jordan Cooper, James Anderson).
3. **Illustrative Diagrams:** For mobile platforms (iOS Screen Time, Android Digital Wellbeing, iOS Sleep Focus, Android Bedtime Mode) and in-app configuration screens where vendor terms prohibit external reproduction without written permission, original conceptual workflow diagrams have been created and explicitly labelled:
   > **"Illustrative diagram – not a product screenshot"**
4. **Student Submission Notice:** All non-student images and illustrative diagrams contain explicit markers in `/docs/SCREENSHOT_GUIDE.md` for student teams to replace with authentic, privacy-safe captures from their personal physical devices prior to final grading.

## 3. Replacement Checklist for Students

Before final project submission (12–13 November 2026), student team members must:
1. Replace diagrams with genuine, full-resolution screenshots taken on their physical test devices (iPhone, Pixel/Samsung, Windows/Mac PC).
2. Follow the privacy checklist in `/docs/SCREENSHOT_GUIDE.md`:
   - No names, emails, avatars, or account numbers visible.
   - Crop or censor sensitive notifications or contacts.
   - Use standard device resolution (minimum 750px width).
3. Update `lastTestedOn`, `lastTestedDevice`, and `writtenBy` in `src/data/guides.ts`.
